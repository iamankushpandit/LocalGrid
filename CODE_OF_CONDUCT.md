# Code of Conduct

LocalGrid adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other project space.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and
remember that many contributors are hobbyists working on their own time with their own hardware.
Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/iamankushpandit). Reports are handled in
confidence. The maintainer may remove content, or block people from the project, as the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
